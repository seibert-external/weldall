Add owner-managed Google connections for Gmail and Calendar, with encrypted server-side credential storage, administrator configuration through the UI and IaC, and CLI commands to connect, inspect, reconnect, disconnect, and proxy requests through Weldall.

Existing CLI workflows, including connector-free IaC, remain compatible with servers that do not support connectors. Connector commands and connector IaC declarations require the updated server.
